What we’re building

Four pieces of one quiet system.

Each one solves a gap we see every day in how Nepalis reach care — at home, in the hills, and far from home.

A first conversation, in the language you think in.

Describe what’s wrong by voice or text. The assistant asks the follow-up questions a doctor would, then tells you how urgent it is and where to go.

  • Nepali first, with English when you want it
  • Built to flag red-flag symptoms early
  • Every pathway reviewed by clinicians

Lighter tools for the people already doing the work.

Female Community Health Volunteers are the backbone of care in rural Nepal. We’re digitising their rounds — simply, offline-first, on the phones they already own.

  • Works on low bandwidth and older phones
  • Visits logged in minutes, not ledgers
  • Escalation to a clinician when needed

How it works

Three steps. One person in the loop, always.

AI does the patient listening and the sorting. Clinicians make the calls that matter.

01

Ask

Speak or type in Nepali. No forms, no medical vocabulary required.

02

Assess

Guided questions build a picture and an urgency level, checked against clinician-built pathways.

03

Connect

To the right next step — self-care, a nearby facility, a health volunteer, or a doctor here or abroad.

Nepali first

Designed in Nepali, not translated into it.

Clinicians decide

AI assists. Diagnosis and treatment stay with people.
